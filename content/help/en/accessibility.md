# Accessibility
> Sessional aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA, and lets each person adjust it to their needs.

## For each person
Open your account menu (the round button at the top right) and use **Display**:
- **High contrast**: stronger text, borders and focus outlines, and underlined links, on top of the school's design.
- **Larger text**: everything 25% bigger, without breaking the layout on a phone.

These choices are kept on that device. If your computer is set to ask for more contrast, Sessional follows it automatically.

## For the whole school
**Settings → Design** has a **High contrast** preset. Every design is checked for contrast; colors that would be hard to read are adjusted.

## Keyboard and screen readers
Every page can be used with a keyboard: **Tab** moves forward, and the first **Tab** offers **Skip to the content**. The chair's console has keyboard shortcuts (listed under **Keyboard shortcuts** on the **Speakers & caucus** tab). Pages use headings, landmarks and labels so screen readers can navigate them, and live changes (the timer, saving, results) are announced.

## Sounds and motion
Every sound has a visual signal. Animations follow your device's "reduce motion" setting.

## How we check
Each release is checked automatically against WCAG 2.2 AA on the pages people use most, in the normal look and with high contrast and larger text, and the rest is reviewed by hand. If something doesn't work for you, please tell us through the contact form.
