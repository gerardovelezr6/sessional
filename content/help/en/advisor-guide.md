# Guide for faculty advisors
> For the adults responsible for the conference and for students' data.

## Your responsibilities in Sessional
You (or another adult) are the school's **Owner** or **Advisor**. You decide who has access, what is public, and what the school's settings are. High Command members are students: they run the conference, but the standard roles give them no staff powers.

## Set up once
1. **People.** Invite your High Command and chairs. Check the **Owner** page: it lists which roles hold staff permissions.
2. **Access.** Decide whether the conference pages need an **Access code** (**Owner → Access code**), and how people sign in (**Settings → People and sign-in**: password rules, Google or Microsoft sign-in).
3. **Public pages.** Choose what the public sees (**Settings → Public pages**). Students' names and grades are never public.
4. **Consent and legal.** If your school has its own privacy policy, terms or a code of conduct, add them under **Owner → Legal and consent** (on plans that include it).

## Students' data
- Sessional keeps what the conference needs: names, emails of people with accounts, delegations, grades and the conference's records. Delegates need no account.
- Nothing personal appears in public pages, addresses or logs.
- **Owner → Export & import** downloads everything (JSON, CSV, PDF) at any time.
- If the school is deleted, it stays recoverable for a retention period, then is removed for good.

## Oversight
- The **Audit log** records who changed what, including any action by Sessional's support (which needs a reason and is time-limited).
- **Usage** on the billing page shows committees, delegates and staff against your plan.

## Money
**Owner → Billing**: plans, invoices and the payment method. Cancelling offers the export first and takes effect at the end of the period.

## Getting help
[The help center](/help), the **Help** link in the app, and the contact form on [sessional.app](/). For urgent problems during a conference, check [the status page](/status) first.
