# Plans and billing
> Plans, contracts, organizations, limits, invoices and cancelling.

## Plans
Plans differ in how many committees, delegates and staff they allow, and in features such as your own domain, your own sender and white-label. **Owner → Billing** shows your plan, what you use, and the options in US dollars or Mexican pesos. Starter and Institution are paid monthly; Conference is paid per year or once for a single conference.

## Contracts
Institution and Circuit can also be taken on a contract of 24 or 36 months, at a discount shown on each button. A contract can't be canceled or moved to a smaller plan before it ends, but it can move to a bigger one. Afterward the plan continues at the regular price until you change it. The billing page shows when your contract ends.

## Circuit: one organization, several schools
Circuit is for organizations that run several schools or conferences. One account pays for all of them on one invoice, and the price per school drops at the volumes shown on the plan. Whoever subscribes manages the organization: **Owner → Organization** lists its schools with what each uses, adds a school (it is billed from the day it is added), copies settings from one school to others as drafts, and adds other managers.

## Paying
Choose a plan; payment goes through Stripe, a secure payment provider. Sessional never sees your card. When you come back from paying, the billing page confirms the payment with Stripe at once. If it still looks out of date, press **Check payment status**. Invoices are listed on the billing page and can be downloaded. Taxes are added where they apply.

## Referred by a partner
If a partner's link brought you to Sessional, or you typed their code when signing up, their discount is applied by itself the first time you pay. The billing page says so before you choose.

## Limits
When you approach a limit, the billing page and the relevant page say so. At the limit, adding more is refused with a clear message; nothing already there is removed.

## If a payment fails
You keep full access during a grace period while Stripe retries. After it, the school becomes read-only: everything can be seen and exported, nothing can be changed, until the payment goes through.

## Cancelling
**Owner → Billing → Cancel**. The export is offered first. You type the school's name to confirm. The plan stays active until the end of the period (or of the contract), and the cancellation can be taken back until then.
