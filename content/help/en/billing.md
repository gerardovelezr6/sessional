# Plans and billing
> Plans, limits, invoices and cancelling.

## Plans
Plans differ in how many committees, delegates and staff they allow, and in features such as your own domain, your own sender and white-label. **Owner → Billing** shows your plan, what you use, and the options in US dollars or Mexican pesos, per year or per conference.

## Paying
Choose a plan; payment goes through Stripe, a secure payment provider. Sessional never sees your card. Invoices are listed on the billing page and can be downloaded. Taxes are added where they apply.

## Limits
When you approach a limit, the billing page and the relevant page say so. At the limit, adding more is refused with a clear message; nothing already there is removed.

## If a payment fails
You keep full access during a grace period while Stripe retries. After it, the school becomes read-only: everything can be seen and exported, nothing can be changed, until the payment goes through.

## Cancelling
**Owner → Billing → Cancel**. The export is offered first. You type the school's name to confirm. The plan stays active until the end of the period, and the cancellation can be taken back until then.
