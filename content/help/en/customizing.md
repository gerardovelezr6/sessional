# Making it yours
> Design, words, languages and rules, previewed before anyone else sees them.

## How changes work
Every setting has a **draft** and a **published** version. Save a draft, turn on **Preview** to see the whole site with your drafts, then **Publish**. Every published version is kept in **History and files**. **Restore this version** brings any earlier one back, as a new version.

## Design
**Settings → Design**: a preset (Sessional, Classic conference, Classic formal, Modern rounded, High contrast or Playful school), then colors, fonts, corners, density, header and tab styles. Colors are checked for contrast as you choose them; one that would be hard to read is adjusted, and the page tells you. Upload your logo and icon in the same section. Advanced users can add custom CSS; anything unsafe or unreadable is refused with the reason.

## Words
**Settings → Words**: what you call a committee, a chair, a delegate, High Command and an advisor, in each language. The new words appear everywhere, including emails and printouts.

## Languages
**Settings → Language** chooses the languages people can use and which comes first. **Settings → Translations** lets you change any text of the app, and add another language.

## Rules of procedure
**Settings → Rules of procedure**: one of four presets, or your own: attendance states, speaking times, motions and points with their order of precedence, voting methods and majorities, and discipline.

## Committee types and grading
**Settings → Committee types** (Security Council with veto, crisis committees with confidential topics, and so on) and **Settings → Grading and awards** (rubric, weights, scales and award rules).

## The community gallery
**Owner → Community gallery** has designs, rules and rubrics other schools shared, reviewed by Sessional. Installing one makes a draft you check and publish. You can share yours too.
