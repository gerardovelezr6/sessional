# Your data and privacy
> Your school owns its data. What we keep, who can see it, and how to take it with you.

## What belongs to whom
Everything your school puts into Sessional belongs to your school. We process it only to run the service. See [the privacy policy](/privacy) and [the terms](/terms).

## What we keep
Names and emails of people with accounts; delegations (country or role, and optionally a name); the conference's records (sessions, votes, documents, grades); and the audit log. We don't sell data, show ads or track people across sites.

## Who can see it
Only members of your school, according to their roles. Each school's data is sealed off from every other school's at the database level, and this is tested automatically. Sessional's support can access a school only with a reason, for a limited time, and it is recorded in your audit log.

## Export
**Owner → Export & import** downloads everything as JSON (which can be imported into a new school), CSV and PDF, at any time, including after cancelling.

## Deletion
Write to us from the contact form or your Owner's email to delete the school. It is kept recoverable for a retention period, in case it was a mistake, and then removed for good. Backups expire on their own schedule.

## Minors
Many users are students. Delegates need no account. Nothing personal appears on public pages, in addresses or in logs, and the gallery never shows a person's name.
