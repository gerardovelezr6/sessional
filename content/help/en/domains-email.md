# Your own address and emails
> Serve the conference from your school's domain, and send emails from it.

## Your own domain
**Owner → Domains** (plans with custom domains). Add an address such as `mun.yourschool.edu`. The page shows the DNS record to create (a CNAME, or an A record for a domain without a subdomain) next to what a live lookup finds right now. Once they match, the domain works over HTTPS; the certificate is issued automatically. Make it the main address and your Sessional address will redirect to it.

## Emails from your school
Invitations, password resets and "you were added" notes are sent in your design and your words: edit them in **Settings → Email** and preview them in **Owner → Email**. Replies go to your reply-to address.

On eligible plans, emails can come from your own address (for example `conference@yourschool.edu`). Enter the domain; the page lists the DNS records (DKIM and SPF) to create and checks them live.

## Branding
On plans with white-label, **Settings → White-label and sign-in** removes "Powered by Sessional" and lets you word your sign-in page.
