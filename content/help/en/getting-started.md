# Getting started
> From signing up to a conference you can try, in about fifteen minutes.

## 1. Sign up
On [sessional.app](/) choose **Start a free trial**. Give your name, your email and your school or conference's name. An adult (a teacher or advisor) creates the school; students join later by invitation. We email you a link. Open it, choose your school's address (for example `colegio-norte.sessional.app`) and a password. The trial lasts 14 days and needs no card.

## 2. The setup wizard
You land in a four-step wizard. Everything in it can be changed later in **Settings**.

1. **Conference**: its name, dates, venue, time zone and the languages people can use (English, Spanish or both).
2. **Look**: a design preset and, if you like, your brand color.
3. **Rules**: the rules of procedure (Standard UN-style, THIMUN-style, Harvard-style or Spanish-language rules) and the words you use for roles.
4. **Start**: load the demo conference, import your delegations from a spreadsheet, or start empty.

## 3. Try the demo
The demo has several committees with delegations, a schedule and documents. Open a committee from the home page and press **Run session**: you can take roll call, keep a speakers list, vote and grade. When you're ready for your real conference, **Owner → Reset** removes every conference (the demo included) and keeps your settings, design, roles and people.

## 4. Tours
The first time you open the app, a short tour shows what your role does. You can close it at any time and bring it back from **Help** in your account menu.
