# Grading and awards
> Grade delegates during the session; totals and award suggestions are ready at the end.

## Grading during a session
In the console, open **Grading**, choose the delegation, the kind (speech, question, document…) and the level. Each grade is one press, with an optional note. Chairs see only their own committees' grades.

## The gradebook
Each committee's **Grades** tab shows the standings, the totals per category and the full log. **High Command → Participation & grades** compares committees.

## Awards
The rules in **Settings → Grading and awards** suggest who earns Best Delegate, Outstanding Delegate, Honorable Mention and Best Position Paper. You confirm or change each, and certificates are printed from **Print**.

## Export
Download grades as a spreadsheet (CSV or XLSX) or PDF. Saved column mappings keep your exports the same every year.
