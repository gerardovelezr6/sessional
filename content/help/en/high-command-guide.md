# Guide for High Command
> For the students who run the conference: before, during and after.

## Your role
You run the conference: committees, chairs, the schedule and announcements. You can't change the school's settings, billing or other people's passwords; your advisor or Owner can. Everything you do is recorded in the audit log.

## Before the conference
1. **Committees.** In **High Command → Committees & chairs**, add each committee with its type, topic and language. Drag them into order.
2. **Delegations.** **Import** reads your spreadsheet (CSV, Excel or Google Sheets). It shows a preview with the rows it can't read and why, imports the good ones, and can be undone.
3. **Chairs.** Assign each chair to their committees. Ask your advisor to invite anyone who isn't in **People** yet.
4. **Schedule.** **Schedule** lists every session, ceremony and break; the home page and public pages show it.
5. **Print.** **Print** makes placards, sign-in sheets, agendas and certificates in your school's design.
6. **Rehearse.** Run a session in a committee with your chairs a few days before.

## During the conference
- **Live analytics** shows every committee at once: in session or not, roll call, the current speaker, votes.
- **Announcements** appear on everyone's home page and on the projection screens. You can schedule them.
- **Confidential topics** (crisis committees) are revealed when you decide, or at the time set in the rules.
- If a chair's device fails, another chair of the committee can open the console; nothing is lost.

## After the conference
- **Participation & grades**: check the standings and confirm the awards.
- **Print** the certificates.
- **Conferences** archives this year's conference and starts next year's, copying its committees and settings if you want.

## Good habits
- Don't share accounts. Each chair signs in as themselves.
- Keep the projector computer signed out; screens are paired, not signed in.
- If something looks wrong, check [the status page](/status) and tell your advisor.
