# Projection screens
> Show the speaker, the timer and the vote on the room's projector.

## Pair a screen
1. On the computer connected to the projector, open your school's address followed by `/display` (for example `colegio-norte.sessional.app/display`). It shows a six-letter code.
2. In the chair's console, open **Screens**, type the code, give the screen a name ("Main hall") and press **Pair screen**.

The screen switches to your committee by itself and stays paired after a restart.

## What it shows
The current speaker and timer, the speakers list, caucuses, votes and their results, and announcements. The screen follows your school's design, with its own projection style (light or dark) under **Settings → Design**.

## Kiosk mode
Use the browser's full-screen mode. The screen hides menus, keeps the display awake where the browser allows it, and reconnects by itself after a network drop, showing the last state meanwhile.

## Sounds
The timer plays a sound when time is almost up and when it ends. Every sound also has a visual signal, so nothing depends on hearing it.
