# Public pages, live view and check-in
> What delegates and families see without an account.

## Turning them on
**Settings → Public pages**. Choose what to show: delegations, schedule, passed resolutions, and the live view. Nothing is public until you turn it on.

## What is never shown
Students' names, grades, notes, confidential topics and unfinished documents. Delegations are shown by country or role only.

## Live view
Delegates follow their committee on their phones: who is speaking, the time left and the speakers list. It works on any phone, without an app or an account.

## QR check-in
**Print** makes placards and sign-in sheets with QR codes. A member of your staff, signed in, scans the code with their phone's camera to check the delegation in.

## Your own address
Delegates who open your school's address are taken straight to the public pages. Staff sign in from there.
