# Running a session
> The chair's console: roll call, speakers, motions, voting and discipline, even on bad wifi.

## Open the console
From the home page, press **Run session** on your committee. Press **Open session 1** to begin. The console has tabs: **Roll call**, **Speakers & caucus**, **Motions & points**, **Voting**, **Discipline**, **Grading** and **Screens**. On a phone, each is a full screen.

## Roll call
Mark each delegation **Present** (or **Present and voting**, or absent). The quorum and the majorities update as you go.

## Speakers and caucuses
Add delegations to the speakers list, press **Next speaker** and **Start**. The timer warns before time is up, with sound and a visual signal. Moderated and unmoderated caucuses have their own timers and lists.

## Motions and points
Record a motion or point. The console orders motions by precedence, tells you which need a second, and runs the vote.

## Voting
Choose the method (roll call, placards, secret) and record each vote. The result follows your rules: simple or two-thirds majority, abstentions, and the veto in a Security Council.

## Discipline
Warnings add up as your rules say, up to losing the vote. They can be cleared.

## When the wifi drops
Keep working. Changes wait on your device, the console says how many are waiting, and they are sent once the connection is back, in order and only once. Nothing shows as saved before the server confirms it.

## Keyboard shortcuts
Open **Keyboard shortcuts** on the **Speakers & caucus** tab: next speaker, start or pause the timer, reset it, and end the speech.
