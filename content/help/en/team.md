# Inviting your team
> Roles, invitations, and who can do what.

## Roles
A new school has one role, **Owner**. Under **People**, press **Add standard roles** to add the usual set:

- **Advisor**: faculty who oversee the conference. Sees everything, changes settings and people.
- **High Command** (or whatever your school calls it): the students who run the conference. They manage committees, the schedule and announcements, but not the school's settings, billing or people's passwords.
- **Chair**: runs the committees they are assigned to, and grades them.

High Command members are usually students, so the standard set gives them no staff powers. The **Owner** page shows which roles hold staff permissions, so you notice if a student role was given too much. You can change any role, or create your own, in **High Command → Roles & permissions**.

## Inviting someone
In **People**, write their name and email, pick a role and press **Create invitation**. If email is set up, they receive the invitation from your school. You can also copy the link and send it yourself. The link works once and is not used up by chat apps or email scanners that preview it.

If the person already has a Sessional account (from another school or last year), they get access at once and can sign in with their usual password.

## Chairs and committees
Assign chairs to their committees in **High Command → Committees & chairs**. A chair sees and runs only their own committees.

## Delegates
Delegates don't need accounts. They follow their committee on the public pages and the live view (see [Public pages](/help/public-pages)).

## When someone leaves
Remove them from **People**. Their past actions stay in the **Audit log**, under their name.
