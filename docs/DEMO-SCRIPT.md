# Sales demo script

A 20-minute demo for a Model UN advisor or High Command, plus 10 minutes of questions. It runs on a
fresh trial school, so the prospect sees exactly what they would get.

## Before the call (10 minutes)
1. Sign up a new school at `sessional.app` with a demo email ("Colegio Demo"). In the wizard pick
   the prospect's language, a design close to their colors, and their rules (THIMUN, Harvard, UN or
   Spanish-language). Load the demo.
2. Open three windows side by side: **Owner** (signed in), **Chair console** (a second browser,
   signed in as a chair you invited), and a **projection screen** at `/display`, paired from the console.
3. Have the prospect's logo ready (PNG). Have their delegation spreadsheet if they sent one.

## The story (20 minutes)

**1. "It's yours in minutes." (3 min)**
Show the school's address and home page. Upload their logo in Settings → Design, change the brand
color, turn on Preview, then Publish. "Nobody sees it until you publish; every version is kept."

**2. "Your rules, your words." (3 min)**
Settings → Rules of procedure: show their preset; open motions and their precedence. Settings →
Words: rename "Chair" to what they say ("Mesa", "Dais") and show it change in the console.

**3. "A session, live." (6 min)** The core of the demo.
- In the console: roll call (mark five present; quorum and majorities update).
- Speakers: add three, Next speaker, Start. Turn to the projection window: the speaker and timer
  are on the screen, live.
- Motion for a moderated caucus, then a vote by placards. The result follows their rules.
- Pull the network cable or turn off wifi on the chair's laptop: keep working, show "Saving 2
  changes", reconnect, and everything is sent once. "Conference wifi is bad; this was built for it."

**4. "Grading without spreadsheets." (3 min)**
Grading tab: three grades in a few taps. The committee's Grades tab: standings and suggested awards.
Print → Certificates in their design.

**5. "Delegates, families and the public." (2 min)**
Turn on public pages. Show the live view on a phone: no app, no account, no students' names.

**6. "Your data, your control." (3 min)**
Owner → Export & import: download everything. The audit log. Roles: High Command members are
students, so they don't get staff powers by default. Accessibility: Account menu → Display → High
contrast and Larger text.

## Questions they usually ask
- **Price?** Owner → Billing shows the plans in USD or MXN, per year or per conference. The trial is
  14 days and needs no card.
- **Our own domain and email?** Yes, on Conference and Institution plans (Owner → Domains, Owner → Email).
- **Import from last year?** Import reads CSV, Excel and Google Sheets with a preview and undo;
  next year, Conferences → Start a new one copies committees and settings.
- **Is students' data safe?** Each school is sealed off at the database level and tested on every
  change; delegates need no account; nothing personal is public. See the help center's "Your data
  and privacy" and `SECURITY.md` for technical buyers.
- **What if the internet fails?** The console keeps working and catches up; screens keep the last
  state and reconnect. `/status` shows the service's state.
- **Can we leave?** Export at any time, including after cancelling.

## After the call
Send the trial link (they keep the school you set up if they want; Owner → Reset clears the demo),
the help center link (`sessional.app/help`) and the two guides (High Command, advisors).
